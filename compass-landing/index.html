[01] MANIFESTO · FOUR PILLARS

A finance cockpit built around the way money actually arrives.

The spreadsheet doesn't know when you get paid. The budget app doesn't know that Friday's check has to last until the check two Fridays from now. Compass does — because the pay period is the unit of truth, and everything else hangs from it.

Built for the next paycheck, not the next month. Mom-grade clarity at every surface, world-class depth under the hood.

[P1] · D11

Period, not month

Everything is anchored to your pay schedule — biweekly by default. The cockpit shows period 14, May 3 → May 17, 5 days remain, not "May 2026". Period close writes a snapshot; the next period is a clean slate.

[P2] · D14

Vessels, not buckets

Seven planetary vessels with plain-English names: Rent, Groceries, Buffer, Utilities, Growth, Joy, Debt. Every dollar has a home. The planetary glyph is visual — the labels are mom-grade.

[P3] · D12

Plan-as-policy

Set the allocation plan once. The L1 routing engine fires on every paycheck, silently, with no confirm modal. The audit log records the action. To change behavior, edit the plan — not your Friday night.

[P4] · D4 + audit

L1 rules + audit log

The L1 engine computes. The LLM advises. The LLM never writes to your ledger. Every automated action is journaled: amount, source, rule fired, when. The receipt is always one click away.

[04] TWO-TIER AI · OUTSIDE THE MATH PATH

Two models. Right job. Never conflated.

The L1 engine writes. The LLM advises. Compass runs a two-tier surface — a production-grade tier for the work that has to be right (onboarding extraction), and a lighter tier for the daily "ask me anything" — read-only, fast, and over your data, never your ledger.

TIER 1 · EXTRACTION

Mavis-grade

Onboarding. Heavy edits. Rule proposals.

Mavis

  • Period → 14 days · derived from pay schedule
  • Rent rule → $1,400 → ☉ Sol vessel · day 1
  • Groceries rule → $480 → ☽ Luna vessel · per period
  • Plan proposal → 40/20/18/8/6/4/4 across 7 vessels
Confirm with one tap. The L1 engine takes it from there.
